Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our sourcing, danedar texture, storage & delivery.

The distinct granular (Danedar) texture is the hallmark of genuine Bilona ghee. When pure clarified butter cools slowly in ambient temperatures, fatty acid triglycerides crystallize naturally into delicate golden granules. Commercial factories artificially homogenize or quick-freeze their products, which eliminates this authentic texture.
Mum Organic Ghee has a natural shelf life of 12 to 18 months at room temperature. Because moisture has been completely simmered away during handi clarification, it does not require refrigeration. Simply store in a cool, dry pantry and always use a clean, dry spoon to prevent water contamination.

4 Home Tests for Pure Desi Ghee

1 The Palm Melt Test (Body Temperature)

Place 1/2 teaspoon of Mum Organic on your palm. Pure ghee has a melting point of 32°C–35°C and melts immediately from human body warmth without leaving sticky wax residues.

2 The Pan Heat & Aroma Test

Heat 1 spoon of ghee in a hot pan. Pure ghee melts instantly and turns a rich golden-brown hue in 10–20 seconds with a rich roasted nutty aroma. Adulterated ghee turns pale yellow with a synthetic smell.

3 The Iodine Starch Test

Melt a spoonful of ghee and add 2–3 drops of tincture iodine. If it turns blue or purple, starch or boiled potato was blended in. Mum Organic remains golden yellow!

4 The Freezer Layer Test

Put melted ghee in a small glass in the freezer for 1 hour. Pure ghee solidifies into a uniform granular single layer. Adulterated ghee with palm oil separates into two visible distinct color layers.
